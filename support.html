Support

Polyclick is a layered metronome for macOS 26 and later.

Getting started

  • Each row is a layer: pick a sound, a subdivision (♩ ♪ 3 ♬ 6) and click steps to cycle Normal → Accent → Ghost → Off. Right-click a step to set a level directly.
  • Space starts and stops. T taps the tempo. ↑/↓ change the tempo by 1, ⇧↑/⇧↓ by 10. R resets a speed-trainer drill. M mutes.
  • Help ▸ Polyclick Help (⌘?) lists every shortcut.

Speed trainer

Every N bars the tempo changes by the step you set until it reaches the target; then it holds, stops, or loops back to the start tempo.

Gap trainer

N audible bars followed by M silent bars, repeating. The beat display keeps running during silent bars.

Output device

Settings ▸ Audio lets you send the click to any output device and, on multichannel interfaces, to a specific output pair, without changing the system default. If the device is missing the app falls back to the system default and says so.

Sounds

File ▸ Import Sound… accepts WAV, AIFF, CAF, MP3 and M4A up to 60 seconds. Imported sounds are copied into the app's library.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: check Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Output device and the master volume slider in the Extras card; the mute button is M.
  • "The audio system is not responding": another app or a misbehaving audio driver has stalled CoreAudio. Click Retry; if that fails, log out and back in or restart the Mac.
  • Flash and click out of sync on Bluetooth speakers: adjust Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Visual calibration.
