Polyclick

Layered metronome & trainer for the Mac.

Polyclick is a metronome built from layers. Instead of one click and an accent, you stack sounds: woodblock on the quarter notes, a hi-hat ghosting the off-beat eighths, a clave pattern on the sixteenths — each layer with its own sound, subdivision, dynamics, gain, pan and swing. Click a step to set it to accent, normal, ghost or off. The result is a click track that fits the music you are practicing, not the other way around.
