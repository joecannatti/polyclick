Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Polyclick does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • The app has no account system and no sign-in.
  • The app makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting frameworks.
  • Your presets, settings and imported sounds are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's sandboxed container, and are included in your own backups (Time Machine, iCloud Drive if you back up your Mac that way). You can delete them at any time by deleting the app.
  • Audio files you import are copied into the app's container and never leave your Mac.
  • The app does not use the microphone or camera.

If a future version ever changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the version's release notes.

Questions: see the support page.
